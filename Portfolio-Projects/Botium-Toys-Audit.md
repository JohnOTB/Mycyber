# Portfolio Project: Botium Toys Security Audit

## Overview
As part of the Google Cybersecurity Certificate portfolio activities, I conducted an internal security audit for **Botium Toys**, an expanding e-commerce toy retailer. 

## Objectives & Frameworks Used
* **Framework:** National Institute of Standards and Technology Cybersecurity Framework (NIST CSF).
* **Focus Areas:** Asset management, risk assessment, compliance frameworks (PCI DSS, GDPR, SOC), and internal controls.
* **Deliverable:** Completed controls and compliance checklist paired with strategic security recommendations (Least Privilege, Separation of Duties, Asset Valuation, and Data Protection Policies).

*Note: The complete checklist document is stored in this repository.*
